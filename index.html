Case Study 01 · Research & Software Award Winner

MarkMe — Proximity-Based Smart Attendance System

Offline-first native Android
The Problem

Academic institutions lose significant pedagogical time to manual roll-calls and proxy sign-ins. Existing RFID or GPS solutions fail indoors or burden students with friction.

The Architecture

Built a native Android client leveraging Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) peripheral beacons. Deployed dynamic rolling UUID cryptographic rotation to eliminate replay attacks, combined with strictly calibrated RSSI signal boundaries (-68 dBm cutoff).

The Results

Awarded 1st place in the college research & software development competition. Instantaneous verification under 350ms with reliable offline SQLite Room caching synced opportunistically to Firebase.
